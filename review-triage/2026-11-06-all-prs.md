## 5. Highest-severity findings

- **#10812 (high, safe-API UB):** child and parent textures share clear views, but every `Texture::drop` destroys them. Dropping a child of a `RENDER_ATTACHMENT` or depth parent frees the parent's clear views; a later clear uses freed views and the parent drop frees them twice.
